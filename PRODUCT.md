# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS — single `index.html`, no build step. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Users

**Primary:** Academic hiring committees — faculty search chairs, department heads, and review panels evaluating candidates for lectureships, research positions, and post-doctoral roles. They scan quickly, looking for fit signals: research area, publication record, h-index, funded project leadership, and teaching breadth.

**Secondary:** EU project partners and collaborators seeking to verify credentials and research overlap before proposing joint work.

## Product Purpose

Personal academic CV website for Marios Touloupou, Ph.D. Its job is to surface the right evidence for a hiring decision — fast. Success means a committee member leaves convinced of research depth, teaching competence, and EU project leadership without having to work for it.

## Positioning

A single source of truth for Marios's academic identity: Post-Doctoral Researcher (CUT / HYPER-AI), Adjunct Lecturer (AUB Mediterraneo, Neapolis), and Innovation Engineer (Innov-Acts Ltd) with a focused research agenda in AI-driven resource optimization, cloud–edge orchestration, serverless computing, and blockchain systems. 743 citations, h-index 16, i10-index 23 (Nov 2025).

## Operating Context

Viewed on a laptop or desktop by a committee member who may have 20+ CVs to review. Likely opened from an email link or a search result. Print-to-PDF must remain viable for dossier compilation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- All content lives in `index.html`; no CMS or data layer
- External fonts and CDN resources are acceptable (GitHub Pages serves them fine)
- Must be print-friendly for PDF dossiers
- No contact form needed — email and LinkedIn links are sufficient
- All factual content (names, dates, project figures, citations) must be preserved verbatim

## Brand Commitments

None. The existing teal palette (`#2A7B88`) is a Word template artifact and is explicitly replaced by this redesign.

## Product Principles

1. **Signal over noise** — the metrics and project leadership are the credentials; they must be immediately visible, not buried in prose.
2. **Earned authority** — everything on the page is backed by verifiable evidence; no invented claims.
3. **Scannable depth** — structure rewards both the 30-second skim and the 10-minute deep read.
4. **Print parity** — the page degrades gracefully to PDF without losing information.
5. **Academic register** — confident and precise, not promotional; let the record speak.
